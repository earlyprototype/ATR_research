# Window-grid experimental results (EXP_010c family + EXP_010d) — extraction and assessment

## SUMMARY

The window-grid programme loops GPT-2 Medium's residual stream between arbitrary layer pairs (inject i -> extract j) to a cosine-gated fixed point, decodes the terminal, and maps which windows produce "rich" (multi-terminal, whole-word, prompt-dependent) behaviour vs single-token funnels. Across ~1,100 gated runs (all on 25-prompt subsets), the record establishes: (1) only the full stack 0->23 produces the Stage-1 `D` collapse; (2) exactly two window cells — O8 (8->21) and A4 (10->21) — carry a via-tail-robust whole-word signature, and they are isolated single-layer islands, not a band (i=9 between them funnels to fragments — H12 REFUTED, robust to a disjoint prompt subset and a stopping-rule change); (3) the band window does NOT recreate GPT-2 Small's basin geometry (H11 REFUTED in EXP_010d, robust across clustering thresholds), while the `D`-collapsed baseline unexpectedly DOES partition prompts significantly like Small (ARI 0.200, p=0.0009); (4) later controls show the `D` collapse itself is conditional on the layer-0 slot plus a ~218x over-natural energy convention. Record-keeping is unusually honest — pre-registered outcome tables, mechanical verdicts, recorded deviations, self-corrections — but the programme's headline positive signals rest on a logit-lens readout whose designated arbiter (EXP_013m J-lens re-decode) has not run and is already known to be a possibly-blind instrument (gate MARGINAL, census found no band). Session notes and results files are largely consistent; the main divergences are the 07-23 note's enthusiasm ("temporal-durative cluster... echoing Small's till basin" — the Small echo was later refuted at partition level) and unpropagated caveats in the rescued EXP_010d record.

## KEY FACTS

- **Registered full run (2026-07-23):** 25 prompts x 6 arms, max_iter=1000, 150/150 converged. A0 (0->23): `D` x25, 4 tensor basins. A4 (10->21): 12 tensor basins, 3 decode terminals (`until` x19, `forever` x5, `since` x1), via-tail agreement 23/25, margins mu 4.20 / max 7.15 — the only arm with non-trivial j<23 agreement above 0/25 (RESULTS_EXP010C.md lines 107-137).
- **Boundary scan (010c-2):** 8 arms x 25, 200/200 converged. Whole-word prompt-dependent terminals occur only at i in {8,10} with extract 21; O8 = `simultaneously` x17 / `halfway` x8, via-tail 17/25 (lines 158-233).
- **In-fill (010c-3, 2026-07-24):** 9 arms x 25 = 225/225 converged. Zero new cells pass the mechanical whole-word flag; 6 of 9 cells funnel to a single terminal across all 25 prompts (`oooooooo`, `<|endoftext|>`, `GOP`, `'d`, `Fas`, `Bhar`); I9 (9->21) = `oooooooo` x18 / `…` x7, whole-word 0/25 (lines 323-451).
- **Via-tail inversions:** X817 `GOP`->`since`, X1015 `Fas`->`the`, X1017 `Bhar`->`Indian`, all 0/25 agreement — direct logit-lens decode fully contradicted by the motor-tail readout at three cells (lines 389-393, 419-422).
- **Follow-up checks (010c-3b, 2026-07-25, issue #21):** funnel-geometry test — no funnel token wins >=1% of 10,000 isotropic directions (collective 0.01%); post-hoc natural-decode — no funnel token appears in the model's natural states at the extract layers (lines 543-608). Disjoint-subset rerun: H12 refutation SURVIVES (I9 `oooooooo` x22, whole-word 1/25) and A0 gate re-passes (`D` x25) (lines 624-645). Seed variation is a no-op — the harness has no stochasticity; "single seed" caveats should read "single prompt subset" (lines 612-622). Stopping-rule check (check_start 100 vs 10): I7 and X1017 identical terminals 5/5 each; I9 0/5 — terminal identity is stopping-rule dependent at slow-drift cells (lines 721-749).
- **Cross-experiment observation:** the whole-word / not / whole-word alternation at i = 8/9/10 reproduces on subset B, a fully disjoint 25-prompt set shared (verified equal) between 010c-3b and 010c-ROBUST (lines 646-662).
- **PERM (2026-07-25, issue #7):** A4's terminal type set {until, forever, since} passes an anisotropy-matched permutation null at p=0.00090 (z=+4.69, Bonferroni alpha*=0.00556) in both readouts; O8's and A5's sets are at/below null means; contrast A1_tail is a near-miss at p=0.00620 (lines 799-855).
- **ROBUST (2026-07-25, issue #11):** 225 runs. Seed variants byte-identical (protocol deterministic). Subset B: A4 keeps 2 of 3 registered types (`until` x22), O8 stays whole-word (`simultaneously` x18) — "same-basin-structure (stable)" and "zone-stable at i=8" readings obtained (lines 867-937).
- **VARIANTS (2026-07-25, issues #13/#14):** Hook-point HP9 (10->21 at resid_post of block 9) identical to A4 on all 9 fields x 25 records. Injection at layer 1 instead of 0 removes `D` (1->23 gives `name` x22). Under natural-energy normalisation A0 and A1 have 0/25 convergence and `D` is absent; measured registered-convention i=0 injection is ~218x natural norm. Combined statement: "`D` has been observed only under {layer-0 resid_pre} AND {~218x natural energy}. Removing either condition removes `D`. The A4/O8 word-window landscape is unchanged under: seed variation, disjoint prompt subset, hook-point convention, and i-scale energy normalisation" (lines 946-1018).
- **EXP_010d (2026-07-24, rescued 2026-07-27 from unmerged PR #5):** Small native loop on the same 25 prompts: 18/25 converged, terminals `prolet` x13 / `Divine` x7 (non-converged) / `till` x3 / `Anarch` x2, 7 basins. A4 vs Small ARI 0.048, p=0.244 — non-significant at every threshold 0.99-0.9995. A0 vs Small ARI 0.200, p=0.0009 — significant at 0.995/0.999/0.9995 (RESULTS_EXP010D.md lines 36-90).
- **J-lens cross-reference (addendum 2026-07-25):** Medium's J-lens census found zero lens-dominant layers; at i=8/9/10 the census reads J agree-final 0.02/0.02/0.02 — "8 and 10 are indistinguishable from 9 and from their flanks" under that instrument; the 07-23 expectation that the two instruments localise the same band "is NOT borne out on Medium" (RESULTS_EXP010C.md lines 768-791). EXP_011m and EXP_013m: "not run" (RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md status line).
- **Recorded deviations/bugs (a partial but representative list):** prompt library absent, subset re-derived from Stage 1 record (line 29-33); model from pre-2020 HF S3 mirror with provenance caveat (lines 37-47); container restarts mid-run in the full tier, the in-fill, and ROBUST V3, each recovered via per-arm checkpoints and merged artifacts (lines 96-100, 336-340, 883-890); lag_scan placeholder bug found in PR #4 review, full+scan tiers regenerated with zero terminal mismatches (lines 279-317); "7 of 9" single-terminal count corrected to 6 after PR #33 review (lines 405-408); E23 (10->23) found to flag under the uniform rule and added to the hand-off list after being omitted (lines 664-685); ladder-8 characterisation added after PR #33 noted the map advertised columns for never-characterised cells (lines 696-703); infill issue header said 10 windows/250 runs, table enumerated 9/225 — ran the 9 justified cells (lines 343-347); VARIANTS executing agent twice killed by server-side API errors, analysis finished by the orchestrating session (lines 951-953); EXP_010d wheel-only pip install deviation (RESULTS_EXP010D.md lines 25-29).

## CLAIMS AND HYPOTHESES

1. **H9 (collapse rescue)** — "at least one window with 0<i or j<23 produces multiple terminals, word terminals, or non-convergence." Made: RESULTS_EXP010C.md lines 140-144. Status per source: SUPPORTED. Assessment: well supported by artifacts (results_full.json cited; 150/150 runs, several multi-terminal arms). The criterion is weak (almost any deviation satisfies it), which the record does not hide.
2. **H9a (band placement)** — the most-different-from-baseline window sits in the mapped band. Lines 144-151. Status: "SUPPORTED on the registered wording, with a recorded measure caveat" — "most different" had no pre-registered metric. Assessment: honest about the metric gap; A4 is the outlier on four convergent measures, but this is the softest verdict in the file and the caveat (window length co-varies) is real.
3. **H10 (edge localisation)** — landscape changes non-uniformly as an edge slides. Lines 183-189. Status: SUPPORTED. Assessment: supported; the scan table shows qualitative changes between adjacent settings.
4. **H10a (final-layer necessity)** — REFUTED in strong form: re-including the tail (10->22, 10->23) does not restore `D`; only 0->23 of 14 windows produces `D`. Lines 189-207. Assessment: supported by the table; the record's own CAUTION (the splice story "absorbs both confirmation and refutation") is exactly right and was TC-prompted (07-23 session addendum).
5. **H10b (sensory-splice necessity)** — SUPPORTED: 0->21 and 4->21 collapse to `,` with no motor involvement. Lines 209-211. Assessment: supported, but later VARIANTS Control A shows the 0->23 `D` does not survive i=1 while 0->21's `,` does — H10b "inherits this caveat" (line 977); the i=0 slot is doing work the original verdict attributed to the splice.
6. **The word-forming islands (A4 10->21, O8 8->21)** — the central positive claim: two cells with >=2 unique whole-word prompt-dependent terminals, via-tail robust, highest margins. Made throughout; map at lines 459-510. Assessment: the best-supported positive result in the programme. Survives disjoint subset (ROBUST V3), hook-point convention (HP9 identical), energy normalisation (class unchanged), and the vacuous seed axis. Two open holes: stopping-rule stability at A4/O8 was proposed but never tested, and the J-lens census provides zero corroboration.
7. **H12 (injection-zone continuity)** — REFUTED: i=9 between the islands funnels to fragments; 8->21 and 10->21 are separate one-layer islands. Lines 424-430; pre-registered refutation branch verified verbatim in EXP_010c3_SPEC.md line 126. Assessment: the most robust negative in the record — survives a disjoint prompt subset (whole-word 1/25) and a stopping-rule change (class stable even though I9's terminal identity flips completely). Solid as scoped to the logit-lens readout.
8. **H12a (zone edges)** — "SUPPORTED on the onset side, with a recorded sharpening"; the sub-prediction "7->21 whole-word but 5->21 not" failed (neither is whole-word). Lines 431-439. Assessment: the SUPPORTED grade is defensible against the spec's outcome row but generous; the record does state the failed sub-prediction plainly.
9. **H13 (extraction ladder)** — SUPPORTED; edge at j=21, sharp; robustness does not extend one layer below. Lines 440-446. Assessment: supported; the via-tail collapse below j=21 is strong. Item 4 adds j=21 is a sharp peak from above too at injection 8 — with a recorded, unexplained asymmetry (E23 at injection 10 flags whole-word; E822/E823 do not).
10. **Funnel/terminal-token claims** — "the single-token funnels are not shown to be a decode-geometry artifact... they remain a property of the looped dynamics." Lines 543-608. Status per source: mechanical middle-row verdict ("quantitative, partial, no verdict beyond the numbers"), S2 sub-condition not literally satisfied (2/6 tokens outside central 90%, both low-norm); the decisive-looking natural-decode check is explicitly POST-HOC with "no verdict weight." Assessment: the evidence genuinely disfavours the artifact reading, and the discipline in not upgrading a post-hoc check is commendable; note the 07-25 session note rounds this up to "funnels not a readout artifact" (see CONFLICTS).
11. **H11 (band window recreates Small geometry)** — REFUTED (robustly) in EXP_010d: A4-Small ARI 0.048, p=0.244, non-significant at all four thresholds; "Escaping Medium's `D` collapse produced a *different* geometry, not Small's" (RESULTS_EXP010D.md lines 78-90). H11a (basin count) also REFUTED for A4 (12 vs 7, beyond k=4). Assessment: the refutation is robust and well-evidenced. The eye-catching converse — A0 (all-`D`) partitions like Small, ARI 0.200, p=0.0009, threshold-robust — is correctly labelled observation-plus-interpretation, but is fragile: 18/25 of A0's prompts sit in one cluster, Small's reference includes 7 never-converged `Divine` points, single subset, and the terminals inherit the (later-discovered, never-back-propagated) stopping-rule caveat.
12. **"Medium tensor finding" synthesis (SESSION_2026-07-27)** — hypothesis, explicitly "NOT a finding": "the `D` collapse may be an apparatus mask, not the phenomenon," combining (1) A0-Small tensor ARI (PR #5), (2) `D` convention-bound (energy), (3) `D` slot-bound (layer-0). Arbiters named: EXP_013m (issue #46), EXP_011m (issue #45), and an unfiled natural_i ARI re-run with discriminating outcomes stated in advance. Assessment: the three inputs are each documented in merged results records; the synthesis is properly fenced and lists alternatives that would kill it. But all three arbiters are outstanding, EXP_013m is pre-declared ambiguous-on-null (MARGINAL gate, no census band), and the natural_i ARI test is still not filed anywhere in the repo. The hypothesis is currently unfalsified because it is untested, not because it survived tests.

## CONFLICTS/CONTRADICTIONS

- **"Three basins" vs 12 basins:** SESSION_2026-07-23 line 32-33: "25/25 prompts across all 7 categories into three basins — `until`, `forever`, `since`". RESULTS_EXP010C line 110: A4 has **12 tensor basins** and 3 *decode terminals*. The session note conflates decode types with basins — the exact distinction (distinct tensors, shared decode) the results file elsewhere treats as load-bearing (A3, E823, Stage-1 Divine).
- **"Echoing Small's `till` basin"** (same session line 33): EXP_010d subsequently showed A4 matches Small at chance at every threshold (ARI 0.048, p=0.244). The echo was refuted at partition level; the session note was never annotated. (The PERM test did later vindicate A4's terminal-set relatedness — but relatedness among its own tokens, not similarity to Small.)
- **"Two instruments ... should localise the same band"** (07-23 session, arc item 4): refuted — the census found no band and no {8,10} vs {9} distinction; the results file records the failed expectation explicitly (lines 786-791). Recorded contradiction, correctly handled.
- **Session 07-25 "Two were strengthened (funnels not a readout artifact; ...)"** vs the results file's deliberately partial item-1 verdict ("quantitative, partial, no verdict beyond the numbers"; S2 sub-condition unmet; decisive check post-hoc with "no verdict weight"). The session-note phrasing is a stronger claim than the mechanical record supports.
- **Stale "single seed" caveats:** item 2a (07-25) established the seed caveat is vacuous, yet RESULTS_EXP010D.md (rescued verbatim 07-27, i.e. after the discovery) still says "Single seed; one 25-prompt subset" (line 123) — a known-wrong caveat ported into the canonical record two days after it was corrected elsewhere.
- **Stopping-rule caveat not propagated to EXP_010d:** the ARI partitions are computed on terminal tensors; item 5 shows terminal state can still be moving at "converged"; EXP_010d's caveats do not mention this (the run predates the discovery, but the 07-27 rescue and the 07-27 synthesis both cite it without adding the caveat).
- **Corrected-count residue (resolved):** the in-fill's "7 of 9 single-terminal" was corrected to 6 in both the results file (lines 405-408) and the session note (lines 42-43) after PR #33 review — a real error, but transparently patched with no verdict impact.
- **Issue header vs executed grid (recorded):** issue #6 said 10 windows/250 runs; 9/225 ran, with the justification recorded (lines 343-347).

## RED FLAGS

- **The baseline is apparatus-conditional.** The whole grid is framed against Medium's `D` collapse, and three reproduction gates "PASSED" on reproducing it — yet VARIANTS shows `D` exists only under {layer-0 resid_pre slot} AND {~218x over-natural injection energy} (lines 1013-1018). Every early verdict phrased against "the collapse" (H9, H10a, H10b) quietly inherits this conditionality; the record states it flatly but no early verdict was re-graded.
- **The arbiter may be blind, and everything defers to it.** EXP_013m is named the "registered arbiter" for every mid-stack claim roughly 15 times, but the Medium J-lens gate validated MARGINAL and the census found no coherent band, so the 07-27 note pre-declares a null "ambiguous between 'no latent content' and 'instrument can't see it'". A deferral chain ending in an instrument that cannot deliver a clean negative is a structural weakness of the whole programme.
- **No independent corroboration of the islands.** The census reads i=8/9/10 as indistinguishable (agree-final 0.02 each). The programme's flagship positive result is currently a single-instrument (logit-lens + via-tail) measurement of the looping procedure itself.
- **A4/O8 terminal stability untested.** The 07-25 session note itself: re-decoding "assumes the terminal is a stable object; item 5 shows that assumption is cell-dependent and currently untested at the cells that matter most." No spec, issue, or results section for this check exists in the repo.
- **The capstone nearly fell out of the record.** The A0-Small ARI observation lived only in stranded, unmerged PR #5; the 07-27 note had to plead "Operator action wanted: rescue PR #5." The rescue happened (RESULTS_EXP010D.md lines 136-149), but a single-human bottleneck on record integrity is now demonstrated.
- **The 07-27 synthesis's third arbiter is vapor.** The natural_i ARI test — the only proposed experiment that directly discriminates the mask hypothesis — was "not yet filed anywhere" and remains unfiled; EXP_011m (#45) and EXP_013m (#46) are both "not run."
- **Verdict generosity at the margins**, each break favouring the story: H12a SUPPORTED despite a failed sub-prediction; H9a SUPPORTED on an unmetricized criterion; PERM contrast A1_tail near-miss (p=0.00620 vs alpha*=0.00556) triggering no downgrade. Individually defensible, collectively a lean.
- **Statistical thinness throughout:** every cell is n=25 prompts (one extra disjoint subset for three cells); ~25 grid cells scanned with 3 flags and no multiplicity control on the flag rule; cluster-threshold sensitivity is a standing "unexplored" caveat everywhere except EXP_010d's own sweep.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Run the A4/O8 stopping-rule stability check before any EXP_013m effort** — it is the cheapest test that could invalidate the flagship cells, it was proposed on 07-25 with a clear rationale, and it has silently dropped off the queue.
2. **File and run the natural_i ARI re-run** (07-27 note, arbiter 3) — it is the only pre-specified experiment that discriminates the mask hypothesis, and its discriminating outcomes are already written down.
3. **Back-propagate corrected caveats:** replace "single seed" with "single prompt subset" in RESULTS_EXP010D.md; add the stopping-rule caveat to EXP_010d's terminal-tensor partitions; annotate the 07-23 session note's "three basins... echoing Small's till" with pointers to the 12-basin count and the H11 refutation (the house rule keeps session notes as journey record — a dated annotation, not a rewrite).
4. **Re-grade or annotate the early splice verdicts (H9/H10a/H10b) against the VARIANTS result** — a one-paragraph dated addendum stating which verdicts are conditional on the j-scale energy convention would prevent downstream readers from citing "the splice drives the collapse" unconditionally.
5. **Decide explicitly what a J-lens-blind Medium means for the deferral chain.** If EXP_013m cannot produce a clean null, EXP_011m (subspace overlap, which bypasses readout rankings) should be promoted to primary arbiter and said so in the results records — the current framing lets every claim shelter indefinitely behind an arbiter that may never adjudicate.
6. **Treat the record-keeping discipline as an asset to protect:** pre-registered outcome tables, mechanical verdicts, recorded deviations, and TC's two register corrections (the anti-validation addenda in SESSION_2026-07-23) are the reason this review can be done at all; the PR #5 near-loss shows the process needs a merge-hygiene rule (no capstone result may live only in an unmerged PR for more than a session).
