# Remaining Experimental Results — Extraction and Assessment

## SUMMARY

Four result sets were reviewed: EXP_010B (GPT-2 Small window grid), the Medium J-lens track (validation gate + EXP_012m band census), and EXP_012-PYTHIA (placebo grid + H8 depth control). All four are pre-registered, mechanically scored, and unusually honest about deviations. The verdicts as stated do follow from the numbers shown. However, the substantive picture is strongly negative for the J-space/workspace programme: (1) on Small, no sub-stack window reproduces the Stage 1 semantic landscape — the full stack is the only window with it, so there is no ATR-side "candidate band" inside the stack; (2) on Medium, the fitted J-lens gate is MARGINAL and the census finds **zero** lens-dominant layers ("NO COHERENT BAND"), with no J-lens correlate of the ATR {8,10}→21 word-cell islands — the two instruments disagree; (3) on Pythia, window-position effects are PRESENT on a placebo model with no known consolidation, which strips the Medium window map of its specificity as band evidence; (4) H8 is SUPPORTED (depth causal for Pythia fragmentation) but under a convention in which depth and injection loudness co-vary 63× vs 403×. The headline "H5-coarse SUPPORTED" is technically correct but the decision rule was nearly unfalsifiable; the finding underneath it is a null for the band story. The plan-level kill conjunction (STAGE2_PLAN.md lines 29-33; RUNBOOK_JLENS_MEDIUM.md lines 85-88) is not yet triggered — everything now rests on the unrun EXP_011/EXP_011m subspace-overlap tests.

## KEY FACTS

- **EXP_010B gate: exact, not close.** SB (0→11) reproduced Stage 1 gated classification "exactly, 25/25" — same tokens, same lock iteration (120) for all 14 converged prompts, 11 Divine non-converged with ` Divine` decode (`RESULTS_EXP010B.md` lines 9-20; gate rule pre-registered in `EXP_010b_SPEC.md` §4 lines 120-126: any mismatch → STOP).
- **EXP_010B H5-coarse rule** (`EXP_010b_SPEC.md` lines 28-34): SUPPORTED if ≥1 of 5 sub-stack windows differs from baseline on *any* of four fields (converged fraction, tensor-basin count, decode class, prompt-dependence); NOT SUPPORTED only if *all five* reproduce the baseline on *all* fields. All five differed on multiple fields (`RESULTS_EXP010B.md` lines 129-141).
- **EXP_010B central observation:** "No sub-stack window reproduces the Stage 1 semantic landscape — the five basins survive no cut tested; the full 12-layer stack is, at this grid resolution, the only window that produces them" (`RESULTS_EXP010B.md` lines 146-149). S1/S4 collapse to a ` the` funnel, S3 to `.`, S2 fragments into 25 private basins, S5 mostly refuses to converge (23/25 decode `<|endoftext|>`, lag-1 *negative* −0.42..−0.25 — a new anti-correlated period-2, not the Stage 1 Divine bell, lines 97-106).
- **EXP_010B energy caveat:** i=0 arms (incl. SB itself) re-inject at 56–175× natural norm; observations are seed_j-convention-dependent until a natural_i variant runs (lines 108-127).
- **Medium J-lens instrument:** anthropics/jacobian-lens pinned at 581d398; 100-prompt WikiText-103 fit, 7h04m CPU, `max_d_mean` 0.58→0.009 (`RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md` §1). Issue #15's claim that the fitting corpus ships in the repo's `data/` was wrong — `data/` is eval sets only; fitting on them would have contaminated the gate (deviation §5.1).
- **Medium gate verdict MARGINAL** (`RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md` lines 147-154): the literal criterion ("at mid layers, J-lens top-5 more interpretable/task-relevant than logit-lens on the majority of test prompts", RUNBOOK_PHASE0 via lines 109-113) "passes decisively at content positions and fails at prediction positions for layers ≲12, passing there only from ~L15." First run, no retries, per the runbook's "FAIL is evidence for EXP_012m and is not retried into passing."
- **Gate table verifies both halves** (`experiments/jlens_medium/gate_table.md`): content positions show real semantic-neighbourhood transport (` sun`→` solar`/` celestial`/` lunar` L10; ` army`→` cavalry`/` generals` L2-10) vs logit-lens BPE fragments; prediction positions L5-12 are dominated by prompt-independent junk stems (` streng`, ` mathemat`, ` cryst` recur across boot/mars/napoleon/jack_jill — a lens-artifact signature). Multi-hop intermediates weak: `Italy` never surfaces on the boot prompt at any layer, either lens.
- **EXP_012m band rule** (pre-registered, `RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md` lines 172-179): layer l is "lens-dominant" iff (i) median rank_J(final top-1) < median rank_LL, (ii) J-better fraction ≥ 0.6, (iii) J-lens top-1 agreement ≥ 0.10. A band = maximal contiguous run of ≥3 lens-dominant layers; none → "no coherent band."
- **Census verdict follows mechanically** (`census_table.md`): no layer passes even criterion (i) — J med-rank is worse than LL at all 23 layers (L0: 154 vs 119; mid-stack J stuck 600–4000 while LL falls 119→1); J-better peaks 0.50 at L0, ≤0.20 elsewhere; agreement ≥0.10 only at L19-22. "Lens-dominant layers: []. Band: NO COHERENT BAND."
- **Multi-hop null on both lenses:** hit@10 ≤0.01 (LL) and exactly 0 (J) at every layer; "At 345M there is no layer at which either readout surfaces latent multi-hop intermediates" (lines 202-207).
- **BOS scoring bug (recorded, deviation §5.3):** the first census run's multi-hop columns scored the BOS token instead of the intermediate (`force_bos=True` side effect); rerun once, every non-mh number bit-identical, band verdict identical under both runs. The superseded first run is disclosed, not hidden.
- **Pythia H8 verdict** (`RESULTS_EXP012_PYTHIA.md` lines 96-121): treatment P-A1 (0→11) converges 25/25 at lock 120, 1 basin, off-diag cos 1.000, single terminal ` bast` ×25; control P-A0 (0→23) converges 13/25, 15 basins, cos 0.486, 11 unique terminals. "H8: SUPPORTED — depth is causal... under the registered seed_j energy convention," with the flat caveat that the two arms inject at 63× vs 403× natural norm so "window depth and injection loudness co-vary."
- **Pythia placebo findings** (lines 137-153): (1) window-position effects PRESENT (converged fraction 13/25–25/25, basins 1–25 across arms); (2) Medium's in-band features replicate only partially (10→21 again gives exactly 3 word-like terminals — "feature coincidence" — but 8→21 gives one shared subword and the highest margins sit off-band); (3) no `D`-class collapse anywhere — Pythia's full stack is the *most* fragmented, not the most consolidated.
- **Pythia P-A1 readout fragility:** the H8 consolidation terminal ` bast` has margin 0.02 and 0/25 direct-vs-via-tail agreement (via-tail decodes `'1'` ×25) — the consolidation claim is solid at tensor level (cos 1.000) but the terminal identity is readout-dependent (table line 62).
- **Recorded Pythia deviations:** sixth window swapped (issue #12 said 8→15; spec pre-registered O8 8→21 before any run); mid-sweep harness fixes committed while sweep ran (process used launch-time code 62ecff3); two commit-subject convergence miscounts (15/25 vs actual 13/25; 18/25 vs actual 17/25) corrected in the register from artifacts (lines 154-169).

## CLAIMS AND HYPOTHESES

1. **"Reproduction gate: PASS" (EXP_010B).** Made: `RESULTS_EXP010B.md` lines 9-20. Status per source: PASS. Assessment: fully supported — 25/25 exact per-prompt match on (`converged`, `terminal_token`) including lock iterations, against a pre-registered mechanical rule with a committed check script and log. Not marginal at all; exact.

2. **"H5-coarse: SUPPORTED" (EXP_010B).** Made: lines 129-154; rule in `EXP_010b_SPEC.md` §1. Status: SUPPORTED. Assessment: the verdict follows trivially from the table — every window differs on multiple fields. But the rule is close to unfalsifiable (one window, any field), so "SUPPORTED" carries almost no information; the informative content is the *direction*: qualitative window-dependence exists, yet no window is "richer" in the semantic sense the workspace frame needed. The register itself fences this ("Anything richer than the verdict line is observation, not interpretation") and states the negative flatly. The "richest window" answer is split (S2 by prompt-dependence with a readout caveat; S5 by dynamics) and "neither matches the baseline's few-shared-semantic character" — i.e., the candidate-workspace-band designation transfers to Phase 2 essentially empty.

3. **"Gate MARGINAL" (Medium J-lens).** Made: `RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md` lines 147-154. Status: MARGINAL (recorded both ways, no retry). Assessment: the split is genuinely in the data (gate_table.md confirms both the content-position wins and the prediction-position junk stems). Two caveats: (a) the pre-registered gate was binary pass/fail — MARGINAL is a post-hoc third category, though transparently constructed; (b) on the STAGE2_PLAN P0-3 wording ("multi-step prompts... more interpretable"), the multi-hop-specific portion arguably *failed* outright (no latent entity recovery anywhere), so MARGINAL is on the generous side. What the doc concludes the lens is good for: transporting *verbalizable semantic-neighbourhood* content at content positions and at L15-22 — "the null is not 'the lens does nothing'" — but not reading a workspace better than the trivial readout, and not surfacing multi-hop content at 345M (lines 261-267).

4. **"EXP_012m: NO COHERENT BAND" (Medium).** Made: lines 197-201, census_table.md footer. Status: null, decided by the pre-registered three-criterion rule. Assessment: fully mechanical and verified against the table — no layer passes criterion (i), so nothing reaches the contiguous-run test. What would have counted as a band: ≥3 contiguous layers each with J median-rank better than LL, J-better ≥0.6, and agreement ≥0.10. Caveat (mine, not the doc's): the census scores only position −1, the position type the gate had *already* shown to be the lens's weak regime below L15; the rule was committed before census output but after the gate result was known (§2 even says "EXP_012m below measures exactly this"). A content-position census was not run, so "no band" is established specifically for prediction-position dominance.

5. **"No J-lens correlate of the ATR {8,10}→21 islands."** Made: lines 226-237. Status: flat comparison, no relationship asserted. Assessment: supported — J metrics at layers 8/9/10 are indistinguishable (agree 0.02/0.02/0.02, med-rank 1240/923/1084), robust to the one-layer convention shift. This is an explicit instrument disagreement: "the two instruments disagree about whether anything distinguishes {8,10} from {9}."

6. **"H8: SUPPORTED — depth is causal for Pythia-410m's fragmentation" (EXP_012-PYTHIA).** Made: `RESULTS_EXP012_PYTHIA.md` lines 110-121; criterion verbatim from RUNBOOK_PHASE1 §EXP_010a via spec §5. Status: SUPPORTED under the registered seed_j convention. Assessment: the mechanical verdict follows on all four pre-registered axes. But the confound is severe and the register says so: both arms inject at i=0 far above natural norm *at different multiples* (63× vs 403×), and EXP_010c-VARIANTS Control B already showed Medium's i=0 landscape class *changes* under natural_i renorm. "Depth is causal" should be read as "the 0→11/0→23 contrast is causal under a convention where depth and loudness co-vary"; the natural_i mirror that would separate them is recorded follow-on, not run.

7. **"Window-position effects PRESENT on the placebo" (Pythia).** Made: lines 137-149, against spec §6's pre-registered reading table. Status: observation only (spec forbids cross-model interpretation in the register). Assessment: supported by the arm table. The spec's own reading: "the Medium map's 'window position matters' component is not Medium-specific." This is the quiet killer for the band interpretation of the Medium map — structured window effects on a model with no consolidation means window effects are not, by themselves, workspace signatures.

8. **Joint effect on the programme.** The STAGE2_PLAN phases that assumed a band: EXP_010c's window choice used the "naive 40-90% mapping (layers 10-21)"; Phase 2 priors (RUNBOOK_JLENS_MEDIUM §3) branch on band existence; Phase 3's H7 asserts "a coherent J-lens band structure exists at 124M-410M scale, and its presence/shape predicts each model's ATR regime." Status now: Medium has no band (EXP_012m), so H7's existence conjunct fails at 345M; the 10-21 mapping has no J-lens support; the ATR islands have no lens correlate; Small has no sub-window that owns the semantic landscape (EXP_010B); Pythia shows window effects are generic. The results doc states the conclusion itself: "ATR and J-lens do **not** localise the same band by independent means on Medium" (lines 266-267). The formal kill conjunction (dead Medium band + dead Small grid + null EXP_011 overlap) is *not* met — EXP_010B's grid is not "dead" (window-dependence exists) and EXP_011/EXP_011m have not run — but the workspace framing now survives on exactly one unrun experiment class (subspace overlap) plus EXP_013's re-decode.

## CONFLICTS/CONTRADICTIONS

- **Instrument disagreement (documented):** ATR in-fill map (#6/PR #33) flags {8,10}→21 as special; the J-lens census sees nothing at 8/9/10 on any metric (`RESULTS_JLENS_MEDIUM.md` lines 226-237).
- **H5 headline vs H5 substance:** "SUPPORTED" (the hypothesis was window-dependence *within vs across band boundaries*) coexists with the finding that there is no band-shaped structure at all on Small — support for the letter of H5, refutation-flavoured for its motivation.
- **Runbook deliverable vs actual:** RUNBOOK_JLENS_MEDIUM §5 specifies final line "MEDIUM J-TRACK COMPLETE — H9-mechanism: <verdict> · EXP_012m · EXP_011m · EXP_013m"; the results doc ships a partial-scope line ("unblocked leg, issue #15") with EXP_011m/EXP_013m "not run (own issue)." Scope split is recorded but the runbook's H9-mechanism verdict slot is unfilled and H9/H9a are referenced in the runbook without definition in STAGE2_PLAN's hypothesis table (H5-H8, Q-D only).
- **Issue text vs execution, twice:** issue #15's fitting-corpus claim was factually wrong (corrected, deviation §5.1); issue #12's sixth window (8→15) was substituted with O8 8→21 by task directive (pre-registered before running, but it is still a divergence between the issue record and the executed design).
- **Pythia P-A0 vs Stage 1:** 13/25 converge here where Stage 1 reported 9/125 and no consolidation — flagged in-register as protocol-difference, "not baseline-anomalous in the spec §2 sense," which is defensible but means the H8 control baseline is not literally the Stage 1 regime.

## RED FLAGS

- **The H5-coarse rule cannot realistically fail.** "≥1 window differs on any field" would be triggered by almost any perturbation of a 12-layer loop. The headline verdict will travel as positive evidence when the underlying result is a null for the band frame.
- **Census nullability was partially built in:** the band rule scores only position −1, already known (from the gate, run first) to be the lens's weak regime below L15. A band defined on content-position metrics was never tested.
- **"MARGINAL" is an invented middle verdict for a binary gate**, and EXP_012m proceeded using a lens that did not cleanly pass "no Stage 2 conclusion uses the Medium lens before this gate passes" (RUNBOOK_JLENS_MEDIUM §2.2). Mitigated by the runbook's fail-is-evidence clause, but the gate's authority was softened in the direction that let work continue.
- **H8's depth/loudness confound (63× vs 403× injection energy)** — the SUPPORTED verdict is convention-conditional and the deconfounding natural_i mirror is deferred. Same class of caveat applies to EXP_010B's SB/S1/S4 (56-175×): the *only* window with the Stage 1 semantic landscape is itself an energy-convention-dependent observation.
- **BOS convention mismatch pending:** the lens was fitted and evaluated with a leading BOS; the ATR engine does not prepend BOS (deviation §5.4). EXP_011m/EXP_013m plan to push ATR terminal tensors through this lens — a distribution mismatch is standing in the pipeline.
- **Pythia consolidation terminal ` bast` (margin 0.02, via-tail agreement 0/25)** — quoting the H8 result as "consolidates to ` bast`" would overclaim; only the tensor-level consolidation is robust.
- **Commit-subject convergence miscounts** on two Pythia arms (corrected from artifacts) — minor, disclosed, but shows headline numbers in the commit stream cannot be trusted without the register.

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Treat "H5-coarse SUPPORTED" and "H8 SUPPORTED" as bookkeeping verdicts; in any synthesis or leadership summary, state the substantive results instead: no sub-window on Small carries the semantic landscape; Medium has no J-lens band; window effects appear on the placebo; multi-hop content is unreadable at 345M by either lens.
2. Before EXP_011m/EXP_013m: resolve the BOS mismatch explicitly (either refit/evaluate the lens without BOS or prepend BOS to ATR terminal decodes) and pre-register which convention governs.
3. Run the natural_i mirrors (Pythia H8 arms; Small SB/S1/S4) before any depth-causality or full-stack-uniqueness claim leaves the repo — both headline positives currently rest on the seed_j convention.
4. If a content-position band census is cheap (it is — same artifacts pipeline, position −2), run it before declaring Medium band-free tout court; the current null is specific to prediction-position dominance.
5. Tighten verdict vocabulary: the gates are binary by design; either add MARGINAL to the pre-registered outcome space in future specs or force the binary call and record the split as observation.
6. Update STAGE2_PLAN hypothesis dispositions now (H7 existence-conjunct failed on Medium; H8 supported-with-caveat; H5-coarse supported-but-band-negative) and make explicit that the workspace framing's survival now depends entirely on EXP_011/EXP_011m — so those should be prioritised and their nulls pre-registered with the same rigour, since per the kill criteria they are the closing move either way.
