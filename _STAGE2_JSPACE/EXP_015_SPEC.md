# EXP_015: does the Small-like prompt grouping survive natural loudness? (pre-registered spec)

**Status:** PRE-REGISTERED. This spec was written and committed before any
analysis output for this experiment existed.
**Created:** 2026-08-01. Chartered by issue #59 under TC's in-session
direction. The test itself, with its discriminating outcomes, was
pre-described in `sessions/SESSION_2026-07-27_MEDIUM_TENSOR_SYNTHESIS.md`
(the "direct test not yet filed anywhere" item) and had no spec until now.
**Identifiers:** EXP_015 and H15, allocated in `REGISTER.md` in the same
commit as this file, per the register's rule.
**Compute:** analysis only. Every input is a file already committed in this
repository; no language model is loaded or run.

---

## 1. The question in one sentence

Does the better-than-chance resemblance between the medium model's baseline
grouping of the 25 test prompts and the small model's grouping, which
EXP_010d measured under the loud injection convention at an agreement score
of 0.200 on the adjusted Rand index scale (0 means no more agreement than a
random grouping, 1 means identical grouping) with permutation p = 0.0009,
survive when the medium model's end states are instead recorded at natural
loudness?

## 2. Background in plain terms

This project repeatedly feeds a language model's internal activity back into
itself until the activity either settles or the iteration cap is reached.
Under the registered convention, the fed-back signal at the medium model's
first layer is about 218 times its natural strength (measured in
`experiments/exp_010c_windows/RESULTS_EXP010C.md`, EXP_010c-VARIANTS
Control B). Under that loud convention the medium model's full-stack loop
collapses every prompt to the single empty token `D`. At natural strength
the collapse disappears: the runs stop settling and the `D` readout is
absent.

EXP_010d then found, as a side observation, that the loud-convention end
states nonetheless group the 25 prompts much the way GPT-2 Small does
(adjusted Rand index 0.200, permutation p = 0.0009, meaning roughly 9 of
10,000 random regroupings matched or beat that score). The 2026-07-27
session note raised the apparatus-mask hypothesis: the suspicion that the
famous difference between the two models is a property of how the apparatus
injects the signal, not of the models themselves. That note named this
comparison as a deciding test and stated its two readings in advance. This
spec freezes that test.

## 3. Hypothesis (H15, continuing the numbering; last used H14)

**H15:** the prompt partition induced by the medium model's full-stack loop
end states recorded at natural injection strength agrees with GPT-2 Small's
reference partition above chance. Operationally: adjusted Rand index
greater than 0 with one-sided permutation p < 0.05 at the registered
clustering threshold (cosine 0.999).

## 5. Method: the EXP_010d machinery, adapted only in what it reads

The method is the registered EXP_010d comparison machinery
(`experiments/exp_010c_windows/compare_small_basins.py` and the `cluster()`
function in `analyze_terminals.py`), with new code confined to input
adaptation: a short script `exp015_natural_ari.py` in the same directory
loads the tensor files named above and calls the existing functions
unmodified. The steps, in order:

1. **Reproduction gate first.** Re-run the clustering and agreement
   computation on the loud-convention A0 end states from
   `terminals_full.pt` against the Small reference. The recorded values
   are adjusted Rand index 0.200 and permutation p = 0.0009
   (`RESULTS_EXP010D.md`). If the recomputed values do not match the
   record within rounding, stop and record the discrepancy: the machinery
   would not be the machinery of record, and the new comparison would not
   be interpretable against the old number.
2. **Clustering.** Greedy leader clustering of the end-state mean vectors
   on cosine similarity at threshold 0.999, the registered
   `CLUSTER_THRESHOLD` in `analyze_terminals.py`, applied to each model
   separately. This assigns each of the 25 prompts a basin label per model.
3. **Agreement.** Adjusted Rand index between the Small label vector and
   the natural-loudness A0 label vector, computed by
   `adjusted_rand_index()` in `compare_small_basins.py`, unchanged. The
   index is corrected for chance and does not depend on how either side
   names its basins or how many basins each side has.
4. **Null.** The same permutation test EXP_010d registered: hold Small's
   labels fixed, shuffle the natural-loudness labels 10,000 times (seed
   42), and read the fraction of shuffles whose index reaches the observed
   value, with the standard add-one correction (`permutation_p()` in
   `compare_small_basins.py`, unchanged). This one-sided p is the
   probability that a random regrouping does as well as the observed one.
5. **Threshold sweep, descriptive only.** Re-cluster both sides and
   re-test at thresholds 0.99, 0.995, 0.999 and 0.9995, matching the
   EXP_010d sweep. The verdict is read at 0.999 alone; the sweep is
   robustness context and carries no verdict weight.

## 6. Pre-registered verdict table

The two readings are copied in substance from the 2026-07-27 session
note's discriminating outcomes, stated there before any of this ran.

| Outcome at threshold 0.999 | Reading |
|---|---|
| Adjusted Rand index > 0 and permutation p < 0.05 | **H15 SUPPORTED.** The Small-like partition does not require the loud convention. The apparatus-mask hypothesis strengthens, because the structure survives with the loud convention turned off, and the founding cross-model contrast more likely reflects the apparatus. |
| Permutation p >= 0.05, or index <= 0 | **H15 REFUTED.** The structure EXP_010d measured is bound to the loud convention, and the apparatus-mask hypothesis weakens. Per the session note, this outcome does not by itself separate injection strength from the mask; the follow-up control in section 7 would be needed before treating it as decisive. |

**Degenerate-partition guard:** if the natural-loudness A0 end states form
a trivial partition at threshold 0.999 (one basin holding all 25 prompts,
or 25 single-prompt basins), there is no substructure to compare, and the
primary comparison is recorded as unanswerable at the registered threshold
rather than forced into a row; the sweep is then reported descriptively.
This guard is not expected to fire: the Control B characterisation
recorded 14 tensor basins for this arm at this threshold
(`RESULTS_EXP010C.md`).

**STOP condition:** a reproduction-gate failure (section 5, step 1) stops
the experiment before the primary comparison is read.

## 7. The mandatory caveat, carried from the session note

At natural loudness the medium model's runs do not settle: 0 of 25 runs
reached the stability criterion (lag-1 cosine 0.999 held over 3
consecutive checks), and every run hit the 1,000-iteration cap
(`RESULTS_EXP010C.md`, Control B). The end states compared here are
therefore snapshots of unsettled trajectories, not settled basins. Three
things vary together in this test: injection strength (loud versus
natural), the presence of the suspected mask (the `D` readout, observed
only under the loud convention), and settledness (25 of 25 settle under
the loud convention, 0 of 25 under natural). No outcome of this test can
attribute the result to one of the three alone. The session note's
follow-up control is restated here as the registered next step if the
resemblance drops to chance: a natural-strength arm engineered to still
produce the `D` readout (natural loudness with forced decode) would be
needed to separate injection strength from the mask.

Standing caveats inherited from EXP_010d: single seed; one 25-prompt
subset; the Small reference itself settled on only 18 of 25 prompts, so
its partition carries possibly-noisy points; partition-level agreement is
necessary but not sufficient for a same-mechanism claim; and the
comparison is partition-level by construction, because the two models'
internal vectors have different sizes (768 versus 1024 numbers) and cannot
be compared coordinate by coordinate.
